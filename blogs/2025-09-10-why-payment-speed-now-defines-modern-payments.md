Key Takeaways Payment speed has become a defining feature of modern payments, shaping trust, efficiency and growth. Real-time rails allow businesses to settle transactions in seconds, reducing risk and enabling seamless customer experiences across industries such as FX, gig platforms, utilities and property. To deliver safely at speed, platforms need event-driven architectures, strong controls and clear user experiences.
