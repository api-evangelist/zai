Key Takeaways Your WooCommerce store’s success does not end at checkout; it depends on what happens after. A poor post-purchase experience leads to churn, while fast delivery, smooth support and proactive communication build loyalty and repeat sales. Merchants who master post-purchase excellence gain higher retention, stronger customer trust and a lasting competitive edge.
